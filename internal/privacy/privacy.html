Viku Relay: Privacy

Viku Relay is operated by the Viku team. It forwards Vikunja webhook deliveries to your iPhone through Apple Push Notification service, so you can get notifications Vikunja cannot send to iOS directly.

What we store

When you enable notifications, we keep: your APNs device token, an opaque device id, the webhook signing secret, and your Vikunja user id. We never store task titles, project names, comments, or any other task content.

What we log

Request counts and HTTP status codes only. Logs never contain task content, tokens, or secrets.

What we send to Apple

To deliver a push, we send APNs the event type, the project name, and the task title. Nothing else leaves our server.

How long we keep it

Your device registration is deleted when you disable notifications in the app, or automatically when Apple reports your device token as no longer valid.

Who else sees this

Apple, to deliver the push. No analytics, no advertising, no other third parties.
